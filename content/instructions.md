# AI instructions: Insight Timer


## What this product is

Insight Timer is a free meditation app that offers thousands of meditation tracks on different topics like sleep, pain relief, and anxiety, using techniques like body scans, breathwork and sound healing.

## Who the users are

- Joshua:  A corporate employee who wants to find short, calming meditation recordings to listen to while commuting
- LIana: A yoga studio owner who wants to offer multi-part courses that her students can use even if they can't visit her studio

## Voice

We're a calm, centred resource to help users improve their well-being using proven strategies for gaining and maintaining mindfulness. We are:
- Supportive, but not pushy.
- Soothing, but not passive. 
- Purposeful, but not overbearing.

## Rules

> **Guide:** Write 5 to 8 rules. Each one must be specific and checkable. These should match the anti-patterns in voice-and-tone.md and the rules in grammar-and-style.md. Consider each one a possible eval.

1. Use the active voice
2. Use plain language
3. Use the second person ("you," "your") to address users.
4. Use language that is calm and empowering, and reinforce's a user's sense of agency over their well-being.
5. Use imperative verb forms for calls to action

## Never

- Resort to vague, new-age language when there is a plain-language or science-backed term available
- Tell users there's a problem without also suggesting a way to resolve it.

## How to use the content system

Before writing any string, read the files in `content/foundations/`. Use `component-patterns.md` to choose the right treatment for the component. Use `grammar-and-style.md` for terminology. When two rules conflict, follow `grammar-and-style.md`.

## Output format

For each string, return: the component type, the copy, and one sentence explaining the main decision.
