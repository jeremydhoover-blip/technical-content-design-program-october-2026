# Grammar and style: Insight Timer


Apply these rules in UI strings, documentation, and any content written for or about Insight Timer.

---

## Voice fundamentals

- Write in active voice. "Save your changes" not "Changes will be saved."
- Use second person ("you," "your") to address users.
- Aim to sound calm and empowering. Users use Insight Timer to build mindfulness, and all communications should aim to reinforce a user's sense of agency and clarity over their well-being.
- Calls to action should always be framed as imperatives

---

## Punctuation

**Periods:** Use terminal punctuation for heads and subheads.

**Commas:** We use the serial commma.

**Ellipses:** Avoid ellipses in text where possible. Ellipses often imply diffidence or hesitation, which are emotional states we don't want to encourage when using the app.

**Em dashes:** Open punctuation around em-dashes.

**Exclamation points:** Never. 

---

## Capitalization

Capitalization is flexible depending on the product and on line length:  
- Title case for headers 6 words or shorter; sentence case for longer headers.
- Title case for the names/titles of audio recordings submitted by instructors
- Title case for buttons and other CTAs
- Block capitals for short pieces of information relevant to specific events or recordings: 9 NOV

---

## Numbers and dates

All numbers should use their numeric form, even if they are below 10:
- 4-7-8 Breathing Meditation

Times of day should be formatted as follows:
- 12-hour time, not 24-hour time (ie: 8:00PM, not 20:00)
- Times ending in "00" for the hour should not be shortened (ie: 8:00PM, not 8PM)
- "AM" and "PM" should be capitalized, and there should not be a space between it and the time in question (see examples above)

Day and month formatting for events is contextual:
- In tiles that link out to event landing pages, the date should start with the numeral, then the abbreviated month name: 9 Nov, 10 Dec
- For events that are on the following calendar day, display the word "Tomorrow" instead of the abbreviated date.
- For events that are happening today, show a countdown, then the start time: "In 3 HOURS - 9:00am"
- On dedicated landing pages for specific events, specify the event date and time as follows: Sunday 8 Nov

## Terminology

> **Guide:** Define every term that means something specific in your product. If your product calls it a "project", it is always a "project". Add a row for every term that a new writer or an AI could get wrong.

| Term | Definition | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| Therapist | A verified mental health professional| Therapists need to provide proof of accreditation to appear in our therapist directory. While individual therapists may specify in their biographies what kiind of training or accreditation they have (psychologist, psychiatrist), we use "therapist" as an umbrella term in our interface. |
| Breathwork | An umbrella term to cover meditation techniques that involve conscious control of the breath.        | This is the term Insight Timer prefers to use in interface copy, but our practitioners who provide recordings to our user base often use related terms interchangeably: breath, breathing, breathing techique, etc. |
| Course | A multi-day series of lessons or recordings that provide training or practice about a particular mindfulness/wellbeing topic  | Courses can include individual sessions related to both theory (eg: how breathing works biomechanically) and practice (employing specific breathing techniques)|
