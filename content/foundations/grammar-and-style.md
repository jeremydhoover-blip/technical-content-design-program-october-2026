# Grammar and style: The Saizen

> **Guide:** 

Apply these rules in UI strings, documentation, and any content written for or about The Saizen.

---

## Voice fundamentals

- Write in active voice. "You updated your favorite artists" not "Your favorite artists were updated."
- Address users in the second person ("you," "your"). Use "you" and "your" to speak directly to the reader and keep the tone conversational and human.
- Use present tense for current states and outcomes. Describe what the system is doing. Do: "Your file is uploading." Don't: "Your file will be ready shortly."
- Limit Calls-to-Action buttons to 2 or 3 sentence-cased words. Start with an imperitive verb. Do: "Create account" "See video" Don't: "Click here to watch video" "Favorite artist list"

---

## Punctuation

> **Guide:** Decide each of these for your product. State the rule and give one example. If a rule has an exception, state it.

**Periods:** Use periods in body copy and multi-sentence lists. Do not use periods in headlines, calls-to-action and simple bulleted lists.

**Commas:** Do not use the Oxford comma unless directed by the Legal department.

**Colons:** Use a colon to introduce a list or to label a field value. Don't use a colon at the end of a label that sits above an input ("Name" not "Name:").

**Ellipses:** Use an ellipses, not 3 periods, when indicating truncated copy.

**Em dashes:** Minimize the use of em dashes and only use in body copy. When using an em dash, always leave a space on either side.

**Exclamation points:** Limit the use of exclamation points to one per article.

**Quotation marks:** Use straight quotes in UI copy. Reserve curly/smart quotes for long-form documentation.

---

## Capitalization

> **Guide:** Use sentence case everywhere, except when indicated otherwise.

    "Add new artist" not "Add New Artist"
    "Sign in" not "Sign In"
    "Delete account" not "Delete Account"

Proper nouns and product names are always capitalized per their official styling.

If album titles and song titles are title cased, match the original formatting.

Feature names: Treat feature names as common nouns, not proper nouns, unless they are officially branded. Write "the dashboard" not "the Dashboard."

---

## Numbers and dates

Numbers: Spell out one through nine unless lifting content that doesn't spell out the number as in a song title like "Love Potion Number 9". Use numerals for 10 and above, and always for measurements, quantities in UI labels, and technical values.

Dates: Use Month D, YYYY for full dates (July 6, 2026). In dense UI contexts, use MMM D (Jul 6). Avoid ambiguous formats like 07/06/26 that will confuse interntional audiences.

Time: Use 12-hour format with lowercase am/pm and a space before it (2:30 pm). Include the timezone if it's relevant to the user's context.

Percentages: Use the % symbol with no space (42%).
