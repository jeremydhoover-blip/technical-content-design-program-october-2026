# Personas: Insight Timer


These are the primary users of Insight Timer. Use them to check whether a string would land: would this person understand it, and would it frustrate them?

---

## Persona 1: Joshua, corporate commuter

**Role:** An end-user who wants to find short, calming meditation recordings to listen to while commuting
**Experience level:** He's familiar with using meditation apps, and has used stuff like Calm and Headspace. He that Insight Timer is free to use, has a wide variety of instructors uploading recordings, and doesn't have specific "courses" or packs he needs to complete, unlike Headspace.
**How they learn:** Josh learns by just trying things out

### Who they are


Josh is a mid-level corporate employee at an insurance company who lives in the suburbs and commutes to his job in a midtown office park using public transit. He's married and has two school-aged children, and between work and home commitments, his commutes are the one part of his day that belong entirely to him. He uses Insight Timer to destress from work, or to build up his mental reserves in the morning.

### Goals

- Regain a sense of agency and control over his day
- Feel like he's getting "me time" in a healthy, constructive way
- Build resilience and reduce anxiety over work

### Pain points

- Lack of time - he needs something that's both effective and quick
- Feeling of overwhelm
- Common misconceptions that people who meditate are power yoga users

### Vocabulary they use


- "I really need some time to myself"
- "My brain is feeling fried today. I really need to breathe."
- "I have about 20 more minutes before my stop, I should put the app on now before it's too late."
- 

### What they need from copy

- A calm, even tone that indicates gentle empathy
- Low-pressure environment
- Minimal focus on goals and streaks

## Persona 2: Liana, a wellness practitioner

**Role:** Yoga studio owner
**Experience level:** Liana offers guided meditation classes at her studio, but is just starting to consider building a digital audience
**How they learn:** By reading and reviewing instructional guides

### Who they are

Liana runs her own yoga studio in a mid-sized city, and has a small but devoted group of students. She's branched off into hosting workshops and guided meditations outside of her yoga classes, and has even attended networking events/conferences meant for other yoga studio owners. She's heard about how other studio owners have built up an online following for their meditation programs through Insight Timer, and wants to start doing the same.

### Goals

- Upload her own recordings easily
- Integrate her Insight Timer profile onto other social media platforms - especially Instagram, which is the primary digital platform for her studio
- Offer multi-part courses and programs that her students can use even if they can't visit her studio

### Pain points

- Not very tech-savvy
- Busy with the other aspects of running her studio business - finding students, paying bills, hiring other instructors
- Wants a platform that integrates well with other tools she's using

### Vocabulary they use

busy, student, course, class, enroll, enrollment, schedule

### What they need from copy

- Clear instructions about how to format and upload recordings
- Information about how to share their recordings on other platforms
- The ability to check in with students and assess progress in courses

## How to use these personas

> **Guide:** Write 3 short rules for how a writer or an AI should apply these personas. Keep the rule about conflicts: what to do when both personas need different things from the same string.

- **When writing:**  Ask yourself which persona is most likely to encounter this string in context. Write for them first, then check whether it still works for the other.
- **When reviewing:** Flag copy for both personas that sounds unclear, uses the passive voice, or doesn't provide a feeling of empowerment
- **When the personas conflict:** Prioritize the rule fo Josh, a typical end user, over the rule for Liana, a teacher
