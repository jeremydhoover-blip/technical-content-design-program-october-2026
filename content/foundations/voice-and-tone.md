# Voice and tone: Insight Timer


Insight Timer is a mindfulness app to help busy adults build mental and spiritual wellbeing. We have the largest library of free guided meditation and music tracks on the planet.

## The voice

The voice is who Insight Timer is, in every context. It stays consistent. Our goal is to meet people where they are on their journeys and help them build good habits for better well-being using proven strategies for gaining and maintaining mindfulness.

**Supportive, but not pushy.** We'll provide recommendations, but we won't tell people what they "must"  or "should" do.

**Soothing, but not passive.** We'll convey information calmly, but we won't downplay important issues.

**Purposeful, but not overbearing.** We'll help people set goals, but we won't be aggressive about it.

**Soothing, but not passive.** We'll talk to you calmly, but we won't downplay important issues.

**Purposeful, but not overbearing.** We'll help you set goals, but we won't be aggressive about it. 

### What the voice is not

- Not sales-heavy: "Act now and purchase a lifetime membership before time runs out!"
- Not toxically positive: "Meditating daily can help you clear your anger and maintain a regular sense of happiness and gratitude."
- Not full of woo: "Using Insight Timer regularly will help align your chakras and encourage abundance."

## The tone by context

Our goal is to help people empower themselves. Whatever the context, we need to focus on how we can help, and whatever next steps the user needs to make in a calm, focused manner.

The voice stays the same. The tone shifts with what the user is doing and how they feel.

### Onboarding
In this stage, users might feel a combination emotions - stress, overwhelm, curosity and hope. We need to focus on the latter (curiosity and hope) to help them understand how we can help them with the former (stress and overwhelm). Because of that, we want to ask, in a gentle manner, what their goals are, what kinds of resources they might prefer, and how much time they're comfortable spending each day. 


**Good:**
> "How can we help?"

**Not:**
> "What problems are you experiencing?"

### Empty states
Empty states are a chance to start over, and potentially find something better or more helpful along the way. They are always an opportunity to connect and reset.

**Good:**
> "Set your intention here"

**Not:**

> "Tell us what your goal for today is"

### Errors

When our users encounter errors, we need to tell them how to fix things or start over without making them feel like they're losing progress. Error messages are an opportunity to provie a sense of agency WITHOUT giving in to a false sense of urgency.

**Good:**
> "This recording is no longer available. You can search our library to find a new one on the same topic. "

**Not:**
> "This recording has been removed."

### Confirmations

One of Insight Timer's key goals is to help people feel a sense of control over their well-being and mental health. Confirmation messages and screens provide a natural avenue to do this in a way that is affirming without being toxically positive.

**Good:**
> "You've successfuly reserved your spot in the "The Joy of Life" retreat hosted by Selena Lael. We look forward to seeing you there. "

**Not:**
> "Wonderful! You've successfully reserved your spot in the "The Joy of Life" retreat. You're one step closer to peace of mind."

## Anti-patterns

> **Guide:** Every anti-pattern here can become an eval in Week 4, so make each one specific enough to check. Use the format: **Pattern**: bad example, then good example.

Avoid these in all copy for Insight Timer:

- **Passive voice**: "This recording has  been removed" becomes "This recording is no longer available"
- **Title case for individual recordings**: "Breath-focused meditation " becomes "Breath-Focused Meditation"
- **Avoidance of contractions**: "In this session, you will learn about" becomes "In this session, you'll learn about"
- **Unnecessary sense of urgency**: "You must meditate every day to build a strong mindfulness practice." becomes "Mindfulness becomes easier with practice. The more frequently you try, the better you'll get at it."
- **Written-out numbers below 10**: "Four-Seven-Eight Breathing" becomes "4-7-8 Breathing"
