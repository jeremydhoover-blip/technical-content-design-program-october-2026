# Before Week 2

---

## Read

Read this short piece on what makes a good commit message:  
[How to Write a Git Commit Message (Chris Beams)](https://cbea.ms/git-commit/)

Focus on the seven rules in the middle of the article. You don't need to memorize them, but come to the session ready to apply them when you commit your changes.

---

## Do

Go back to `content/foundations/voice-and-tone.md`. You filled in the "The voice" section last week.

Read through what you wrote. Pick one section and improve it: tighten the language, sharpen an example, or add something that was missing. Make at least one concrete change.

Then ask yourself: if an AI assistant read this file, would it have enough information to write copy in your product's voice? What's still unclear or generic?

Come to Week 2 ready to talk about what you changed and what you think is still missing.
